Daily targets

Adjustment rules

0.5–0.8 kg/week: keep everything unchanged.

<0.3–0.4 kg/week for 2 weeks: verify tracking and steps first, then reduce ~150 kcal/day or increase movement.

>0.9 kg/week consistently + poor recovery: add ~100–150 kcal/day.

Back issue: avoid heavy deadlifts/squats and any exercise that provokes pain. Use pain-free ranges and seek clinician/physio guidance if symptoms worsen or radiate.

Weight convention

Meat: raw trimmed weight.

Rice: dry weight before cooking.

Potatoes: raw weight. Frozen wedges: frozen weight from the bag.

Woolworths rotisserie chicken: weigh the edible skinless breast/thigh meat after removing skin, wings and stuffing.
